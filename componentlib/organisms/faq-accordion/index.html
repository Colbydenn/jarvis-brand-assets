FAQ

Before you book.

How do I book?

DM the artist directly or use the booking link in their bio. Walk-ins welcome Tues + Sat when chairs are open.

What's your deposit policy?

$100 non-refundable deposit holds the chair, applied to the final cost. 48-hour reschedule notice or deposit forfeits.

Do you do cover-ups?

Yes. Bring a photo to consult — we'll talk what's possible. Most cover-ups need 2–3 sittings.

How do I take care of new ink?

Wash with unscented soap, pat dry, apply thin layer of unscented lotion 3× daily for two weeks. No ocean, pool, or direct sun for two weeks.

Do you tattoo minors?

No. 18+ with valid government ID. No exceptions, even with parental consent — Colorado state law.
