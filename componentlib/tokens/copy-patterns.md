# Copy patterns v0

Templates for hero/CTA/social-proof copy. Replace `{tokens}` per lead.

## Hero headlines

### Pattern A — possession
> `{Verb} {noun} that {qualifier}.`
- "Get tattoos that age like the work."
- "Book treatments that actually deliver."
- "Train workouts that compound."

### Pattern B — local-first
> `{Neighborhood}. {Service}. {Years} years in.`
- "RiNo. Bold blackwork. 12 years in."

### Pattern C — direct address
> `You {pain}. We {solution}.`
- "You hate stiff salons. We cut hair."

## Sub-headers

> `{One-liner that anchors the promise in proof or place.}`
- "By appointment. Walk-ins when the chair is empty."
- "Member-priced facials. Surgeon-trained injectors."

## CTAs (verb-led, ≤3 words)

- Primary: `Book {thing}` / `See {thing}` / `Start {thing}`
- Secondary: `Or {soft action}` (e.g. "Or text us first")
- Avoid: "Learn more", "Click here", "Submit"

## Social proof

> `{Stat} | {Quote} — {name}, {role/source}`

Render as ticker, not paragraph. One per line, monospace, `--type-sm`.

## Footer

- Address line in monospace
- Hours in 24h grid
- One social link, max
